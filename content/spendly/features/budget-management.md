Plan monthly spending and monitor progress with threshold-based alerts.

## Key capabilities

- Set monthly overall or per-category budgets
- Compare actual spending against configured limits
- View progress with visual indicators
- Receive overspending alerts at 75% and 100%

## Typical flow

1. Create a monthly budget from the budget section.
2. Select scope (overall or category-specific) and amount.
3. Track progress through budget indicators.
4. Act on notifications when thresholds are crossed.

## Notes

- Budget monitoring runs in the background at regular intervals.
