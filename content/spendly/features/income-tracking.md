Record incoming money with category context, account destination, and recurring schedules.

## Key capabilities

- Add, edit, and delete income entries
- Assign categories and destination accounts
- Track salary, freelance, investment, and refund entries
- Configure recurring income schedules
- Auto-detect eligible income SMS messages

## Typical flow

1. Open the transaction entry flow and select income.
2. Enter amount and choose category and account.
3. Add recurrence if income repeats on a schedule.
4. Save and verify updates in transactions and analytics.

## Notes

- Refund categories can be used to map expense reversals.
